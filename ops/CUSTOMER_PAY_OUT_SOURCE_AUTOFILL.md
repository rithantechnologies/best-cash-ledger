# Customer Pay Out — automatic Paid from source

## Root cause (October 2026)

The Customers > Card Ledger > Pay Out modal previously chose its automatic Paid from
account **only** from `cardLedger.payInSources` (CustomerLedgerEntryDetail direction
PAY_IN, completed). Most card-swipe customer receivables are generated by CARD_SWIPE,
not Pay In. The customer could therefore have an outstanding balance and even previous
provider-wallet payouts, but the modal said "Auto source unavailable" and remained blank.

A Card Swipe provider / settlement account is **not** proof of which cash/bank/wallet
actually paid the customer. Do not guess the debit account from the card swipe.

## Fix

The customer card ledger now also returns `payOutSources`, read from:

- Actual completed customer payout payments on card-swipe payables, using
  `PayablePayment.sourceAccountId` and payment transaction/date.
- Completed customer payout payments on card-due recovery payables.
- Completed manual customer PAY_OUT ledger entries and their real `financialAccountId`.

This excludes reversed entries and never presents a card-swipe settlement wallet
as if it were the actual payout source.

UI selection order:

1. Most recent actual completed customer payout account in the same
   card/customer scope, on/before the selected business date. Prefer the
   card-specific history over the customer's other cards.
2. If **no** eligible prior customer payout exists, suggest a completed
   eligible Pay In (card-specific first, then customer-wide).
3. If the proposed account is inactive/not authorized, or no previous source is
   available, require manual selection. Do not quietly choose an older unrelated
   account. Staff do not see the owner's Main Cash Reserve in account options.

Display the underlying transaction reference/date and allow Change. If the
source comes from previous PAY_OUT, do **not** send the
`sourcePayInTransactionId` API field (only real PAY_IN transactions qualify).
Never auto-post a payout, modify balances, or bypass cash-session confirmation.

## Verification

- Regression: previous actual PaySwitch payout supplied from payable payment
  source (not swipe settlement), original Pay In still supported, reversed
  payouts excluded.
- Date/card matching: Indian business-date cutoff, no later-date entries, no
  different-card suggestions, editable account source.
- API tests and production builds must pass before deploy.

If a customer has **neither** eligible Pay In **nor** a prior actual payout,
there is no evidence-based source to auto-select; user must choose the funding
account manually. A screenshot without customer identifier cannot establish the
particular customer's transaction history.

## Linked swipe context (2026-10-20)

Opening Pay Out for an unpaid card swipe preselects the original swipe's
customer card, its remaining net payable amount, and the matching payable
allocation. The modal now displays a direct link to the originating swipe and
its provider, gateway, gross swipe amount, customer commission, and net
payable. Example: PhonePe/HDFC RuPay swipe ₹30,000 less ₹900 customer fee
means a ₹29,100 payable.

The **swipe provider** is context only; it does not prove the **Paid from**
cash/bank/wallet account, and must never automatically debit that provider
based only on a card swipe. A prior confirmed real payout can suggest the
funding account, with Change available. An actual cash handover must debit
the physical cash drawer and require cash confirmation; a digital destination
uses the actual digital paying account. The operation still requires explicit
Save and retains full payable allocation and audit linkage.
